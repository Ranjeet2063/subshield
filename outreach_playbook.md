# SubShield: US General Contractor Outreach & Customer Discovery Playbook

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
* **Company Profile**: US General Contractors, Commercial Remodelers, Custom Home Builders.
* **Company Size**: $3M – $25M annual contract volume.
* **Subcontractor Count**: 10 to 60 trade subcontractors on active job sites (framers, roofers, MEP, concrete).
* **Key Decision Makers**:
  * **Controller / CFO / Head of Finance**: Cares about avoiding $5k-$15k insurance audit penalties.
  * **Director of Project Management / Senior PM**: Cares about not halting jobsite work when a sub's insurance lapses.
  * **Office Manager / Risk Coordinator**: The person who currently spends 8-15 hours/week chasing certificates manually.

---

## 2. Lead Generation Channels (Zero Cost / Low Cost)

### Channel A: Apollo.io / Clay Filter Recipe
* **Location**: United States (Top construction growth states: Texas, Florida, North Carolina, Colorado, Arizona).
* **Industry**: Construction / Building Construction / Commercial Contracting.
* **Headcount**: 10 - 50 employees.
* **Titles**: `"Controller" OR "Risk Manager" OR "Operations Manager" OR "Owner" OR "Project Executive"`.

### Channel B: ProView / The Blue Book (Free Public Database)
* Visit `proview.mybluebook.com` (Directory of over 500,000 verified US commercial contractors).
* Filter by Region -> General Contractors.
* Export company website & phone/email.

### Channel C: AGC & ABC Local Chapter Directories
* Associated General Contractors (AGC) and Associated Builders and Contractors (ABC) publish public member lists for every metro area.
