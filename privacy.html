LEGAL COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 | Last Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SubShield ("we," "our," or "the Platform") collects business information solely to provide automated insurance compliance and Certificate of Insurance (COI) verification services for commercial construction companies:

  • Account Data: Business email address, company name, user role, and encrypted password credentials.
  • Insurance Document Data: ACORD 25 certificates, policy declarations, endorsement schedules, broker email contacts, and policy expiration dates.
  • Payment Telemetry: Cryptographic transaction hashes, invoice IDs, and payment statuses. We do not store raw credit card numbers.

2. Confidential Treatment of Insurance Documents

All uploaded Certificates of Insurance, subcontractor declarations, and broker communications are treated as strictly confidential commercial data. We implement tenant-level cryptographic data isolation to guarantee that no organization's compliance data is accessible to any third party.

3. Document OCR & Verification Processing

Documents processed by SubShield's automated OCR engines are analyzed solely for the purpose of validating coverage limits, named insureds, endorsement checkboxes, and expiration dates. Customer documents are never sold, rented, or utilized to train public machine learning models.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Subscribers retain full ownership of their data. Upon account termination, customers may request a complete export of their subcontractor compliance records or permanent deletion of stored PDF documents within 30 days of written request.

5. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, audit requests, or data processing agreements, contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@subshield.ai.
