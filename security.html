Enterprise Security Architecture

How SubShield secures subcontractor insurance records, endorsement schedules, and billing data.

Cryptographic Multi-Tenant Isolation

Every subscribing General Contractor is provisioned with a mathematically unique Tenant ID. All database queries, subcontractor profiles, and audit documents are partition-gated to eliminate any risk of cross-tenant exposure.

FIPS-Compliant Password Hashing

User credentials are encrypted using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations of HMAC-SHA256 and unique 128-bit cryptographically random salts. Plaintext passwords never touch our persistence layer.

256-Bit TLS Encryption in Transit

All HTTP traffic is enforced over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) preloaded, preventing downgrade attacks. Security headers enforce clickjacking and MIME-sniffing protection.

Confidence-Gated Human Review

Our verification engine automatically computes confidence scores for every ACORD 25 field. Any ambiguity in policy limits or endorsements automatically routes to a human underwriter queue.
